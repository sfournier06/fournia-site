N8N is the automation tool serious AI consultants use when Zapier becomes too expensive or too limited. It is a platform that connects a small business's applications to each other (CRM, Gmail, Shopify, accounting) and moves information without manual work. The main difference: N8N can be installed directly on your own server, which means your data never leaves your infrastructure. That is a direct advantage for compliance with Quebec's privacy law (Law 25) when your workflows touch customer data.

## What is N8N actually used for in a small business?

N8N gets your applications talking to each other without someone copying and pasting data by hand. You describe a series of steps ("when a customer fills out this form, create a contact in the CRM, send a confirmation email, add a follow-up task") and N8N runs it automatically.

The tool describes itself as a platform for [traceable automation and AI workflows](https://n8n.io/), with more than 500 integrations with common business applications. The community keeps adding new ones.

A workflow in N8N is a series of visual blocks called nodes. Each node performs an action: read a piece of data, transform it, send it somewhere else. The blocks are connected by drag and drop.

## Which workflows can a Quebec small business automate?

Here are concrete use cases a small business can run with N8N ([detailed examples here](https://nathanibgui.com/blog/cas-usage-n8n-pme-workflows-automatisation/) (in French)):

- Automatic follow-ups with customers or prospects by email based on their status.
- Syncing a CRM with accounting software.
- Processing Shopify orders (confirmation, inventory update, tracking).
- Automatic replies to incoming requests (email, website form).
- Extracting data from invoices or documents into a spreadsheet.

What they have in common: these are repetitive tasks where information moves from one tool to another. N8N automates that transfer.

## What is the difference between N8N and Zapier?

The difference in billing matters most for a small business. [Zapier bills per action](https://zignuts.com/blog/n8n-vs-zapier-2026-comparison), so a five-step workflow uses up your quota five times faster. N8N bills per execution of the complete workflow, no matter how many actions it contains.

On the other hand, N8N is more technical to set up. A small business owner can connect two simple tools alone in Zapier; a complete N8N workflow usually requires help.

| Criterion | Zapier | N8N |
|---|---|---|
| Billing | Per action | Per complete execution |
| Self-hosting | No | Yes |
| Learning curve | Low | More technical |
| Data control | Zapier's servers | Your server (if self-hosted) |

For a small business with complex processes or that handles sensitive customer data, N8N often costs less and fits Law 25 better. Zapier remains relevant for a simple, quick need without a consultant.

## Is N8N free? How much does it cost?

N8N is free when self-hosted in the community edition, and paid in the managed cloud version. When self-hosting, you install the software on your own server and only pay for hosting, [often $5 to $10 per month for a small self-hosted instance](https://northflank.com/blog/how-to-self-host-n8n-setup-architecture-and-pricing-guide).

The cloud version managed by N8N is paid. According to [n8n.io/pricing](https://n8n.io/pricing/), the Starter plan is 20 euros per month (2,500 executions, billed annually) and the Pro plan is 50 euros per month (10,000 executions, billed annually). These prices are in euros and may change: check the current amount in your currency directly on n8n.io/pricing.

One thing to know about the licence: N8N is "fair-code", not strictly open source. Internal commercial use is free; reselling the product as a service requires an Enterprise licence. For a small business that uses it for its own operations, this distinction has no impact.

## Do you need to know how to code to use N8N?

No, N8N works by dragging and dropping visual nodes, and a user can build a simple workflow without writing code. What requires technical skills is self-hosted installation, securely connecting the applications and advanced workflows.

That is why most small businesses have N8N installed and configured by a consultant, rather than starting from scratch in-house.

## Can N8N connect AI agents to my tools?

Yes. N8N includes native nodes for building AI agents through its AI Agents module (see [n8n.io](https://n8n.io/)). An agent based on Claude or ChatGPT can read your data, decide on an action and carry it out in your software.

The advantage of running the agent in N8N: every step is traceable. You see what the agent read, decided and executed, which is essential when customer data is involved.

## Why does self-hosting change everything for Law 25?

Self-hosting means N8N runs on a server you control, not on N8N's servers abroad, so your data does not leave your infrastructure. An automation that processes customer data handles personal information within the meaning of Law 25.

When this information passes through servers outside Quebec (the case with the cloud version), section 17 of Law 25 requires a privacy impact assessment before the transfer. A self-hosted N8N on a server in Quebec or Canada eliminates this risk at the source, because the data does not leave.

Be careful: if your workflow calls an AI API abroad (Claude, ChatGPT), the data can still leave Quebec. Self-hosting N8N handles the transport, not every external call. De-identifying the data before the call remains good practice.

## Should you hire a consultant to install N8N?

For a small business, yes in most cases. N8N is more technical than consumer tools, and self-hosting requires server configuration that an owner usually does not have time to manage.

A consultant deploys N8N once, configures it according to your actual processes, then the workflows run without a monthly per-task subscription.
