## AI consultant on Montreal's North Shore: who to turn to in 2026?

A small business on Montreal's North Shore that wants to bring in AI deals with three types of contacts, each with a distinct role. The AI consultant builds and implements the solution, then trains the team. Economic development organizations guide the business, and Canada Economic Development for Quebec Regions (DEC) can fund part of the project through its IRIA program. The Commission d'accès à l'information (CAI) sets the framework for protecting personal information. The consultant's geographic proximity matters for two specific steps: mapping processes on site and training in person.

The rest of this article details who does what, in what order, and when distance becomes a real factor. For criteria to select a consultant or price ranges, two guides already exist: [how to choose an AI consultant for a Quebec small business](/en/blog/choosing-ai-consultant-quebec-2026/) and [how much an AI consultant costs in Quebec](/en/blog/ai-consultant-cost-quebec-2026/).

## Who does what in an AI project for a North Shore small business?

The roles often get mixed up, which wastes small business owners' time. The following table separates the responsibilities.

| Contact | Their role | What they do not do |
|---|---|---|
| AI consultant | Analyze processes, build the automation or agent, implement it, train the team | Grant funding, settle a legal issue on the business's behalf |
| Economic development organizations (RCM, chamber of commerce) | Point toward resources and programs | Build the solution |
| Canada Economic Development for Quebec Regions (DEC) | Fund certain AI projects through IRIA | Choose the consultant or deliver the project |
| Commission d'accès à l'information (CAI) | Oversee the protection of personal information | Build or fund the solution |

A small business that knows this split knows which door to knock on for each question: technical, funding or compliance.

## Does an AI consultant's proximity change anything?

Proximity does not make an AI consultant more skilled. An automation workflow or an agent connected to a language model's API is built the same way in Blainville, in Laval or in Quebec City. Technical quality is judged on past work, method and how the consultant handles data.

Proximity becomes useful at two points in the project.

### Why is process mapping better done on site?

Process mapping means observing how work really flows: who receives the request, in which software, who follows up, where information gets lost. On site, a consultant sees the parallel Excel files, the sticky notes on the screen and the steps nobody thinks to mention on a video call. These details often decide which automation to build first.

### Why does in-person training help adoption?

An automation the team does not use brings in nothing. Training delivered in the company's offices makes it possible to test the tool on real files, address employees' concerns and spot hiccups right away. For a North Shore small business, a consultant located nearby can come back on site without travel becoming a significant cost item.

## What funding exists for an AI project in Quebec in 2026?

One federal lever for Quebec small businesses is the Regional Artificial Intelligence Initiative (IRIA). According to [DEC](https://www.canada.ca/fr/developpement-economique-regions-quebec/nouvelles/2026/07/intelligence-artificielle-des-investissements-du-gouvernement-du-canada-pour-propulser-des-entreprises-du-quebec.html) (in French), IRIA is delivered by DEC in Quebec until March 31, 2031. The same news release states that DEC is supporting 63 projects, with contributions totalling $13,852,374 to 63 Quebec organizations. The program supports the development and commercialization of AI solutions, as well as the integration of AI into small business operations.

The [IRIA program page](https://www.canada.ca/fr/developpement-economique-regions-quebec/financement-services/initiative-regionale-en-matiere-dintelligence-artificielle.html) (in French) sets out the main conditions:

- **For a small business**, the support is a repayable contribution of up to 50% of authorized costs.
- **For a non-profit**, the support is generally non-repayable and can reach 90% of authorized costs.
- **Municipalities and public bodies** are not eligible.
- **Costs are eligible from the date the application is filed.**

### In what order should you file the application and sign with the consultant?

The IRIA application is filed before signing the contract with the consultant. Since costs only become eligible from the filing date, a small business that signs and pays before filing risks losing coverage for those expenses. The practical order is therefore: scope the project, get the technical description and the quote, file the application, then sign.

### Who can help prepare the application?

Economic development organizations in the region, such as the RCM or the local chamber of commerce, can point a business toward relevant programs. Their services vary: it is best to confirm directly with them. The AI consultant, for their part, can write the technical description of the project and the quote, two documents that are useful for putting the application together.

## Which Law 25 obligations affect an AI project?

An AI project that processes personal information about clients or employees falls under Quebec's privacy law (Law 25). The [Commission d'accès à l'information](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/protection-renseignements-personnels/information-entreprises-privees) (in French) reminds private businesses that they must protect the personal information they hold and manage it properly.

The CAI also details the obligations in the event of a [confidentiality incident](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/protection-renseignements-personnels/information-entreprises-privees/incidents-confidentialite-mesures-securite-entreprises) (in French):

- An incident that presents a risk of serious harm must be reported to the Commission in writing.
- The business keeps a register of all incidents, retained for at least 5 years.

For an AI project, the question to ask the consultant is concrete: where does the data go, who has access to it, and is it de-identified before being sent to a model hosted outside Quebec? A serious consultant answers these questions before starting the build. The details are in the guide on [Law 25 and AI in Quebec businesses](/en/blog/law-25-ai-compliance-quebec-2026/).
