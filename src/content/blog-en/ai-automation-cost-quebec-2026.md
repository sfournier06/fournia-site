## How much does AI automation cost a small business in Quebec in 2026?

In Quebec in 2026, automating a process with AI generally costs $500 to $15,000 CAD for the project (depending on complexity), plus about $0 to $300 per month for the automation platform (N8N, Make or Zapier, in US dollars, depending on the tool and volume) and $300 to $1,500 CAD per month in maintenance if it is outsourced. A simple small business project often lands between $3,000 and $10,000 CAD all in, with a typical payback in 2 to 6 months. Several of these costs can be funded up to 50% by the federal DEC-IRIA program (repayable contribution for a small business).

The price breaks down into three separate envelopes: the build (one time), tool subscriptions (monthly) and maintenance (monthly). Add 30% to 50% in hidden costs. The real question is not the sticker price: it is the ROI and the share paid by government.

## How does the price of an AI automation break down?

The total cost splits into three envelopes that you need to keep separate to compare offers properly.

The first envelope is the build: the project paid for once. It covers designing and implementing the workflow or agent. The second is the platform subscription, billed every month based on the volume of actions. The third is monthly maintenance, if you outsource it rather than keeping it in-house.

These three envelopes do not compare with each other. A low build price with an inflated subscription can cost more over 12 months than a higher build with tight recurring costs.

## What is an AI consultant's hourly rate in Quebec?

In Quebec, an AI consultant charges about $140 CAD per hour on average ([leanware.co](https://www.leanware.co/insights/how-much-does-an-ai-consultant-cost), [aidolsgroup.com](https://aidolsgroup.com/en/blog/category/research-report/ai-consulting-cost-guide/)). Internationally, rates go from $150 to $350 US per hour, and Big 4 firms charge $300 to $600 US per hour ([automatisation-intelligence-artificielle.fr](https://www.automatisation-intelligence-artificielle.fr/cout-automatisation-ia-entreprise/) (in French)).

The hourly rate on its own says very little. What matters is the result delivered per dollar spent. A more expensive consultant who delivers a reliable workflow in two days costs less than a low rate spread over three weeks.

## How much do automation tools cost per month?

Automation platforms bill by the volume of actions processed per month. The three most used by Quebec small businesses are N8N, Make and Zapier.

N8N ranges from $0 (self-hosted) to about €24 per month ([n8n.io/pricing](https://n8n.io/pricing/)). Make starts at $9 US per month for 10,000 operations. Zapier is free up to 100 tasks, then goes up to about $49 US per month on the Pro plan ([toolradar.com](https://toolradar.com/blog/zapier-pricing-2026), [automationatlas.io](https://automationatlas.io/answers/zapier-pricing-explained-2026/)).

The gap widens with volume. At about 10,000 actions per month, Zapier costs around $300 US per month, compared with $50 to $100 US for Make and $5 to $50 US for N8N. For a small business automating several processes, the choice of platform can change the recurring cost by a factor of six.

## Is it better to subscribe to an existing tool or go custom?

Subscribing to a managed tool costs less in the short term. You are live in a few days, often for less than $1,000 in setup ([cipherprojects.com](https://cipherprojects.com/blog/posts/n8n-vs-zapier-automation-tool-comparison/)). It is the right reflex for validating an idea quickly.

Custom becomes worthwhile when workflows are complex, when volume makes the subscription bill explode, or when you want to avoid vendor lock-in (depending on a platform that can change its prices). The most cost-effective approach: validate first with a managed tool, then migrate to custom once the value is proven. You only pay for custom on what already works.

## What are the hidden costs of AI automation?

Three items push the bill past the advertised price: language model tokens, integrations and maintenance. Together, they generally add 30% to 50% on top of the project cost ([thecrunch.io](https://thecrunch.io/ai-agents-price/), [aiessentials.us](https://aiessentials.us)).

Tokens are the usage billed by AI models on every request. The more the automation runs, the more it consumes. Integrations (connecting your CRM, your online store, your internal tools) take configuration time that does not always show up in the initial quote.

You also need to plan for 2 to 4 hours of maintenance per month to keep a workflow reliable. A quote that ignores these items is an incomplete quote, not a cheap one.

## How much does monthly maintenance of an automation cost?

If you outsource maintenance, count on $300 to $1,500 CAD per month depending on the system's complexity ([thecrunch.io](https://thecrunch.io/ai-agents-price/)). If you keep it in-house, plan for 2 to 4 hours per month of your time or your team's.

Internationally, the benchmarks confirm this range: a simple agent costs $300 to $800 US per month and a multi-agent system $1,000 to $3,000 US per month ([insights.reinventing.ai](https://insights.reinventing.ai/articles/ai-agents-cost-performance-smb-2026-04-08)). Maintenance is not an optional cost: an automation without upkeep eventually breaks when a connected tool changes or an unforeseen case comes up.

## What ROI to expect, and how fast?

The typical ROI of a well-scoped AI automation is 200% to 500% in the first year, with payback in 2 to 6 months ([blog.innovate247.ai](https://blog.innovate247.ai/roi-ai-automation-smb-real-numbers/), [insights.reinventing.ai](https://insights.reinventing.ai/articles/ai-agents-cost-performance-smb-2026-04-08)).

A concrete example: a customer service agent that handles 70% of requests can generate about $7,250 per month in net savings, against a subscription of $1,500 per month to run it. In this case, the system pays for itself in a few weeks and keeps producing every month.

That is where the real risk shifts. When a well-scoped project pays for itself in a few months and can be 50% funded, the real risk is not spending: it is standing still while your competitors automate.

## What AI grants exist for a small business in Quebec in 2026?

The [DEC-IRIA program](https://www.canada.ca/fr/developpement-economique-regions-quebec/financement-services/initiative-regionale-en-matiere-dintelligence-artificielle.html) (in French) (federal) can fund up to 50% of authorized costs for a small business, as a repayable contribution (up to 90%, generally non-repayable, for a non-profit). Costs are only eligible from the date the application is filed (see also [hellodarwin.com](https://hellodarwin.com/fr/aide-aux-entreprises/programmes/dec-initiative-regionale-intelligence-artificielle) (in French), [canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/economic-development-quebec-regions/financing-services/regional-artificial-intelligence-initiative.html)).

The NRC's Industrial Research Assistance Program (IRAP) also offers non-repayable contributions for prototyping and integrating solutions ([hellodarwin.com](https://hellodarwin.com/fr/aide-aux-entreprises/subventions-et-financement/intelligence-artificielle/quebec) (in French)). Combined with a well-scoped project, this funding spreads out the project's cost: the IRIA contribution is repaid over time, while the automation produces its gains in hours saved from the day it goes live.

Important: file the grant application before signing the project contract, otherwise the costs may no longer be eligible.

## How much does a simple project cost compared with an advanced one?

A simple project (automating a defined process, for example sorting emails or updating a CRM) costs $3,000 to $10,000 CAD ([automatisation-intelligence-artificielle.fr](https://www.automatisation-intelligence-artificielle.fr/cout-automatisation-ia-entreprise/) (in French), [mindeon.ai](https://mindeon.ai/cout-automatisation-ia-pme-2026/) (in French)).

An advanced project (voice agent, multichannel journey, several connected systems) costs $15,000 to $50,000 CAD ([floatai.ca](https://www.floatai.ca/tarifs) (in French)). The complexity mainly comes from the number of integrations and the level of reliability required, not from the AI itself.

A note on compliance: as soon as an automation processes personal information, Quebec's privacy law (Law 25) applies. That means consent, human approval and data protection. On public data, you have a free hand.
